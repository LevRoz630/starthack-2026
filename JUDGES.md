## Reading the room

**Marc Aeberhard — CEO.** Also sits on the commercial register board as Marc Fabian
Aeberhard, one of four signatories. He will score criterion 1: could this be
piloted with a few relationship managers right after the hackathon? Answer that
before he asks it.

**Michael Nutter, CFA — Chief Product Officer.** The CFA matters: he knows
portfolio construction properly, and he owns what ships. He is the one who will

Three seats, three different questions. The briefing has to answer all of them in
one pass: pilotable (CEO), grounded and legible (CPO), robust (integration).

## What that means for the message

- **Don't sell the model.** Nutter and the MathConsult lineage can do the maths
  faster than us. The model phrases facts; a deterministic engine owns every
  number. Say so explicitly — it is worth points under "no hallucinated content".
- **Answer the pilot question out loud.** "Pilotable with a few relationship
  managers next week" is a quarter of the score.
- **Show the failure path.** Integration and support scores robustness; a demo that
  survives a malformed file is worth more than one more feature.
- **Never say "replaces the advisor."** Their entire positioning is the opposite.
  We hand the advisor the sentence to say.
- **Make it survive scale.** Anything calling a model per position dies at 500,000
  portfolios, and they will know instantly.
