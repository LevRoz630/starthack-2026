## The company they work for

UNRISKOMEGA AG. Swiss fintech, founded 13 October 2016, registered in Kloten
(Weierstrasse 10, 8302), CHF 100,000 capital. A joint venture of MathConsult,
multilateral AG, math link and uni software plus.

Products: URO Core, with URO Advisor (Basic / Pro / Hybrid), URO Self Invest and
URO Self Pension on top. 25+ banks, 500,000+ portfolios, 1,000+ advisors. Built
against FINMA, FIDLEG and MiFID II. Clients include Swiss regional and cantonal

Their own vocabulary: "quantitative depth", "Swiss diligence", advisors who
"advise with substance" so clients "decide with clarity". The team is described as

## The single most useful fact

**Their public positioning contains no AI at all.** Automation appears only in
self-service contexts, never as advisor replacement. A risk-analytics house with no
AI story has come to an AI hackathon — they are scouting where language and
judgment fit on top of maths they already do deterministically.

So: don't sell the model. Put it in a narrow role, let a deterministic engine hold
every verdict, and frame the result as a compliance gap closed — documented,
auditable, reproducible. See [PITCH.md](PITCH.md) for how that shapes the message.
