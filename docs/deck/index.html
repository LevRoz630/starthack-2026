Why this is real, not a demo

Every sentence is computed, sourced, and checked before anyone sees it.

Nothing is invented

465 briefing sentences across 57 clients. None without a source. The model rephrases facts we computed; a claim checker rejects any sentence that adds a number, a cause or a severity word — and the dashboard shows what it rejected.

It says nothing rather than guess

Four of 57 briefings are short a section, because we had nothing real to put there. Ask the phone about gold a client does not hold and it answers “nothing in the data answers this”.

It can run in a Swiss bank

The briefing is written by Apertus, hosted by Swisscom in Switzerland. Client data never leaves the country. Turn the model off entirely and the briefing still works, in fact text.

It leaves the record behind

The call note and the follow-up email are drafted from the facts that were actually on screen during the call. The advisor approves them; nothing advisor-only reaches the client.

Said before anyone asks: the market move is simulated and labelled simulated everywhere, including in the source lines. Sector moves stand in for each security's own move. We have no bond duration, so we never claim a bond price move.
